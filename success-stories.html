01. La Cantonada

BAR · GIRONA

A fresh twist on the classics.

In the heart of Girona we found La Cantonada, a friendly, laid-back bar where good moments always find their place.

Eager to give their drinks menu a fresh new look, they wanted to add options that were different, refreshing and had their own personality.

Fruity arrived to add a splash of colour to the offer, with flavours like Apple, Green Pear and Exotic Mix. An original alternative to go with vermouth, terrace afternoons and those get-togethers that always run a little longer than planned.

Because sometimes, to break the routine, all it takes is a new flavour.

Same corner. Fresh new vibes.

02. Esbojarrats

NIGHT BAR · GIRONA

Good music. Wild nights. Fresh drinks.

When night falls, Esbojarrats becomes one of those places where something is always happening. Music, lights, friends and an atmosphere that makes you forget about the clock.

They were looking for a drinks offer that matched their personality: bold, different and anything but conventional.

With Fruity they found exactly that. A selection of fruity, refreshing flavours that fits the energy of the venue and offers a different way to enjoy the night.

Between DJ sessions, endless conversations and impromptu dance floors, Fruity found its place at Esbojarrats.

Make it a night to remember.

03. Floreixent

MUSIC FESTIVAL · PLATJA D'ARO

Fresh sounds. Fresh vibes.

In Platja d'Aro, amid music, summer and a desire to discover new things, Floreixent was born: a small festival dedicated to giving emerging artists visibility and creating a space where music and creativity take centre stage.

To support this first adventure, they were looking for brands that shared their young, fresh and label-free spirit.

Fruity joined the festival, bringing its flavours to the grounds and becoming part of the experience: from the first concerts to the last toasts at sunset.

A collaboration that celebrates emerging talent, local culture and all those moments that make a festival much more than music.

Let new sounds bloom.
